# Phase 18.6 Handoff - Mobile Watchlist Screen Polish

Completed on 2026-04-24.

## Summary

The mobile `/watchlist` screen now uses the backend watchlist price view instead of the older local-only list stream. It shows watched product price cards with best price, chain/store, deal status, target hits, and missing-price states.

Users can tap a watched product to open `/product` and can remove items through the existing backend delete endpoint.
